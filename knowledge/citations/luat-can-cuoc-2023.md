# Luật Căn cước (Law on Identification), No. 26/2023/QH15

## What This Is

The primary national law governing citizen identification in Vietnam, replacing the earlier Law on Citizen Identity Cards (2014). It is the top-level legal basis for all four procedures in this Knowledge Pack.

## Verification Status

**Not yet verified against the official gazette or National Assembly portal.** The law's existence, number, and effective date are stable, widely-reported public facts as of this Knowledge Pack's authoring. Specific article and clause numbers cited by individual procedure files are placeholders pending confirmation by a qualified legal reviewer — per `metadata/schema.md`'s Knowledge Rules, this is stated explicitly rather than presented as verified.

## Known Provisions (general, unverified at article level)

- Establishes the renamed identity document ("Căn cước" replacing "Căn cước công dân" as the formal term, though CCCD remains the common colloquial name).
- Establishes an age-based mandatory renewal cycle (commonly cited as ages 14, 25, 40, and 60) — **exact article and any recent amendment unverified.**
- Establishes integration with the national population database, affecting which supporting documents are still required in person versus verified digitally — **current implementation status by locality unverified.**

This Knowledge Pack assumes the 2023 Law is the current, correct top-level legal basis for all four procedures and has not been superseded. This assumption itself should be the first thing a legal reviewer confirms.
