# Declaration of Loss

## What This Is

Required only for the Reissue procedure (Cấp lại CCCD). A citizen states the circumstances of losing their card — this is a self-declaration, not proof requiring external documentation in the ordinary case.

## Notes

- Where the loss is reported as a theft, some localities may separately request a police incident report — **exact conditions under which this escalates unverified.**
- Loss of a Căn cước is not itself a criminal matter and this declaration is procedural, not an admission of fault — the tone of any citizen-facing text referencing this document should reflect that per `CITIZEN_CONSTITUTION.md`'s "never judged" principle.
