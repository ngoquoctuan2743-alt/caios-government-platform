# Residence Information Extract

## What This Is

Confirms a citizen's registered residence. Historically a required physical document (the paper "sổ hộ khẩu" household registration book); Vietnam's household registration book was formally discontinued as a physical document as part of the national digital population database rollout, with residence information verified electronically instead in most cases.

## Assumption Made

**This is the single most important assumption in this Knowledge Pack to flag for reviewer verification:** we assume that, for citizens whose residence data is already correctly recorded in the national database, no separate physical document is required for any of the four procedures — the receiving agency looks it up directly. Where a citizen's data is *not* correctly recorded (a known transitional edge case), a physical extract or in-person confirmation may still be required. **Current rollout completeness and any locality-specific exceptions are unverified** and should be confirmed before this assumption is relied upon in a citizen-facing checklist.
