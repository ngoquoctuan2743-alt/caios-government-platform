# Birth Certificate

## What This Is

Required primarily for first-time issuance (a citizen who has never held a Căn cước/CCCD, most commonly turning 14). Confirms identity and citizenship facts the first issuance process needs before any prior ID record exists.

## Notes

- Not required for renewal, replacement, or reissue, since a prior Căn cước/CCCD record already establishes these facts in those cases.
- Increasingly cross-checked against the national population database rather than requiring a fresh physical copy every time — **current implementation completeness unverified.**
