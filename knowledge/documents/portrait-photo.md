# Portrait Photo

## What This Is

A current, standardized portrait photo required for every one of the four procedures in this pack, most commonly captured on-site at the time of application rather than brought by the citizen, alongside fingerprint (and where applicable, iris) biometric capture.

## Known General Specification (unverified at the exact-current-circular level)

- Plain, light-colored background.
- Recent photo, face fully visible, neutral expression.
- No head covering or eyewear, except for documented religious or medical reasons.

## Notes

- Because capture typically happens on-site, "Typical Missing Documents" in the procedure files refers to a citizen *not yet knowing* a photo will be taken there, not to bringing their own photo — this is an important distinction the Citizen Agent should communicate clearly per `CITIZEN_CONSTITUTION.md`'s plain-language principle.
