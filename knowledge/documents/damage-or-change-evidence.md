# Damage or Change Evidence

## What This Is

Required only for the Replacement procedure (Đổi CCCD), and only in the branch relevant to the citizen's specific reason: the damaged card itself (if replacing due to physical damage), or the official record of whatever changed (if replacing due to an information update — name, address structure change, or similar).

## Notes

- This is a *branching* requirement — a citizen replacing a damaged card does not also need change-of-information evidence, and vice versa. The Checklist Generation subsystem (a future sprint) must treat these as mutually exclusive sub-cases of the same procedure, not both required simultaneously.
- Exact list of which underlying changes require which specific supporting document is **unverified** and should be confirmed by a legal reviewer per change type.
