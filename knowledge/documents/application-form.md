# Application / Declaration Form

## What This Is

The standard declaration form used to initiate any of the four procedures in this pack — first issuance, renewal, replacement, and reissue. The form itself does not differ conceptually between procedures; what differs is which supporting documents accompany it and which boxes are relevant.

## Notes

- Increasingly available for online pre-filling via the national public service portal or the VNeID app, reducing in-person form-filling time — **current rollout completeness by locality unverified.**
- No fee is charged for the form itself.

## Assumption Made

This pack assumes one form serves all four procedures with different checkboxes/sections, rather than four distinct forms. This should be confirmed by a reviewer with current access to the actual form.
